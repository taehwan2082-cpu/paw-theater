Paw Theater Automation

Paw Theater runs two YouTube channels that publish Korean and Japanese versions of short videos, only with the original creator's written permission:

  • Korean channel: @pawtheater_kr
  • Japanese channel: @pawtheater_jp

Paw Theater Automation is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It sends permission-request emails from the owner's Gmail account, reads the replies to those emails, and uploads the localized videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. Every upload credits the original creator with a link to the original video.
