Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Paw Theater Automation ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Paw Theater YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in to it with their own Google account.

Google data the app uses

  • Gmail (send, read-only): to send permission-request emails that the owner has approved, and to read replies in those email threads.
  • YouTube (upload, read-only): to upload localized videos to the owner's own channels and to read their upload schedule.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored only on the owner's computer (a local database and files). It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Replies that grant permission are kept as evidence of permission for as long as the related video is published.
  • Access tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Creators we contact

We contact creators only at email addresses they list publicly. If you do not want to hear from us, reply "no" and we will not contact you again. To ask us to remove a video or delete your data, email taehwan8287@gmail.com.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
